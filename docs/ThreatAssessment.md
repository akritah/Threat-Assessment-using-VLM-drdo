# Threat Assessment Framework & Protocols

This document details the structured threat taxonomy, level estimation rules, and emergency dispatch actions mapped by the system.

---

## 1. Threat Levels

The system classifies surveillance events into three distinct threat levels:

| Threat Level | Description | Example Categories | Action Protocol |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Low** | Normal operation, routine public activities, or standard police arrests that are fully controlled. | `Normal`, `Arrest` | Monitor feed. No emergency services required. |
| **Medium** | Suspicious behaviors, property theft, or non-life-threatening violations. | `Burglary`, `Stealing`, `Vandalism`, `Shoplifting` | Dispatch local security or property police to secure the location. |
| **High** | Immediate threat to life, public safety, or property. Severe group hostilities or active hazards. | `Fighting`, `Assault`, `Abuse`, `Riot`, `Shooting`, `Arson`, `Explosion` | Trigger urgent emergency dispatch alerts (SWAT, Police, Fire, Ambulance). |

---

## 2. Emergency Response Mapping

The Gradio dashboard and the alert pipeline map detected activities to specific responders automatically:

*   **🚒 Fire Rescue & medical Services:** Triggers when the activity relates to fire, arson, or explosions.
*   **🚨 Armed Police & SWAT:** Triggers when the activity indicates active shooters, firearms, or high-risk hostile environments.
*   **🚓 Police Patrol & Security:** Triggers for physical fighting, group brawls, riots, or physical assaults.
*   **🚓 Local Property Police:** Triggers for property violations, burglary, retail theft, and vandalism.

---

## 3. Structured Report Format

When a video segment is assessed, it generates a structured report containing the following details:

1.  **Scene Summary:** A concise, single-sentence caption outlining the core action (generated by the fine-tuned LoRA model).
2.  **Observed Activities:** Descriptive breakdown of individuals and objects involved.
3.  **Potential Threat Indicators:** Flagging items like weapons, fire, forced entries, or physical combat.
4.  **Threat Level:** Categorical assessment (Low/Medium/High).
5.  **Reasoning:** Step-by-step logic supporting the level.
6.  **Recommendations:** Emergency services to contact or safety precautions to take.

Reports are saved in multiple formats (JSON, Markdown, and CSV) under `outputs/` for archival and audit purposes.
